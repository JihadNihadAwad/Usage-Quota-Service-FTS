=== The monthly_request_cap count queries, before they were removed ===

Measured 2026-10-20 on the loadtest database, after the request-count
counters were introduced. Until that change, every consume for a plan with
monthly_request_cap configured ran one of these COUNT(*) queries inside
the serialized transaction; the scan grows with the month's accepted rows.

Loadtest shape: the user plans carry all three rules, the account plans
only two - so the user-level count ran on every loadtest consume (small,
~1.2k rows per user), while the account-level count is what a cap-bearing
account plan would have paid. Account 1 had 124,646 accepted requests in
the month at measurement time; user load-001 of that account had
15017 accepted.

The replacement: accepted_requests columns on account_usage/user_usage,
maintained by the same locked UPDATE that charges units - O(1) under the
lock, verified against these same ledger counts by check-invariants.ts
after every stage.
